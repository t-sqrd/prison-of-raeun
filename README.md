# Prison of Raeun

Shows you the quickest way through the Prison of Raeun agility course on The Graveyard (65 Agility). It tells you how many clicks each charged jump needs and keeps track of your laps, XP and loot.

## On the course

Everything you need to click is outlined, coloured by what it's for:

| Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Amber | Shared obstacles that every lap uses. The first one is labelled **Start** |
| Green | The fast route. In each row of three stepping stones, only the real one is outlined |
| Cyan | Charged jumps, labelled with how many clicks they need |
| Red | Where the slow way round starts, so you know what to avoid |
| Pink | The reward chest, until you loot it (65 Thieving) |

The charged jumps take 2-4-3-2 clicks in the first section and 2-4-2 in the last.

## Info box

Shows your current lap, last lap, best lap, laps, XP per hour and sunken treasure, plus a reminder while the chest is still unlooted. Lap times and your personal best come straight from the game's own lap message, so they match what the game says.

You can turn off the whole box, or any line in it, in the plugin settings. To reset the session, shift + right-click the info box.

## Settings

- Outline obstacles and the chest, each with its own switch
- Choose which obstacles to outline: all of them, the fast route, or the slow route. The slow route leaves out the shortcuts, charged jumps and rows of three stepping stones, and outlines only the first stone of the long line, since one click carries you across it
- Click counts and text labels, each with its own switch, if you want outlines only
- Outline width, fill opacity, label size and how far away obstacles are outlined
- Show the fake stepping stones faintly instead of hiding them
- Loot chest before final jump: until the chest is looted, a left click on the last obstacle no longer jumps and it is not outlined. Jump stays in the right-click menu. On by default so you don't forget the chest
- Info box, with a switch for every line
- A colour for each kind of outline
